One thing I was a little uncertain about - are you trying to predict what the call *will* be or *should* be.  Presumably the latter is of more value, in which case you need to incorporate downstream modeling that seeks to establish some causal understanding of the relationship between the play call and the team's performance.  This is *hard*, but you might be able to produce an 80% solution with a naive model.

Let me know what you decide, but it would be wicked cool to have both a prediction of the call that will be made, and a recommendation for the *best* call.
